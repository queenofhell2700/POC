STAR DEMO INSURANCE - HEALTH POLICY TERMS
Policy Number: SDI-100245

1. Waiting Periods
1.1 Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start, except accidents.
1.2 Pre-existing diseases: not covered until 24 months of continuous coverage.
1.3 Specific illnesses (hernia, cataract, joint replacement): 24 months.

2. Exclusions
2.1 Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment unless due to accident.
2.2 Hospitalization primarily for diagnostic evaluation or observation with no active treatment.
2.3 Treatment for conditions arising from non-disclosure of material facts.

3. Sub-limits
3.1 Room rent: 1% of sum insured per day.
3.2 Cataract: up to INR 40,000 per eye.

4. Co-payment
4.1 10% co-payment on every admissible claim.
4.2 Deductible: none.

5. Hospitalization
5.1 Minimum hospitalization of 24 hours is required, except for listed day-care procedures.
5.2 Day-care procedures are listed in Annexure 2.
